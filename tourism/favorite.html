Amboseli National Park

Amboseli National Park is my favorite tourist destination in Kenya.

About the Destination

Amboseli National Park is well known for its large open plains, swamps and acacia woodlands.

Tourist Attraction

There are many tourist attractions in Amboseli Park;
First, there is mount Kilimanjaro, which is the tallest mountain in Africa.
Second attraction, there are the large herds of elephants that amboseli is known for the whole world.
Third, visitors also come to see lions, cheetahs, giraffes, zebras, buffaloes and over 400 types of birds.

Why Visit the Place

You should visit Amboseli because it is one of the best places in the world to see elephants and take amazing photos with Mount Kilimanjaro. You will enjoy watching birds and beautiful sunsets over the plains. It is a perfect place for family trips, school trips and holidays with friends.
