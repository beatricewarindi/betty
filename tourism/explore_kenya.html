Popular Destinations in Kenya

Maasai Mara National Reserve

Home to the Great Wildebeest Migration, Maasai Mara offers the best wildlife safaris in Africa. See the Big five and experience Maasai culture.

Diani Beach

Diani Beach is famous for its white sandy beaches, clear blue waters and water sports like snorkeling and swimming.

Amboseli National Park

Amboseli is also known for large herds of elephants and the best views of Mount Kilimanjaro.

Mount Kenya

Mount Kenya is the second highest mountain in Africa, perfect for hiking, climbing and mountain scenery.
